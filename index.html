Our Story

Good food, shared with family

Kshitij is a 100% pure vegetarian restaurant on the first floor of Western Corridor, Adajan. Our kitchen cooks North Indian, Punjabi and Chinese dishes, from tandoori starters and rich paneer curries to sizzlers, biryani, mocktails and desserts.

Whether it’s a quiet lunch, a family dinner or a big celebration in our banquet hall, we want every table to leave happy.
